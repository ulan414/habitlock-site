FAQ

A miss was counted unfairly. Open that day in the app and tap "Dispute this day" within 14 days. Support will check the raw readings, and the decision will appear on the same screen.

When will I get my stake back? After a successful challenge, to the same payment method within 1–3 business days. The refund status is shown in Profile, under payments.

Can I cancel a challenge? Not once it has started. That is the whole point.

How do I delete my account? Profile → "Delete account". It cannot be deleted while a challenge is running or a refund is in progress. What remains after deletion is described in the privacy policy.
